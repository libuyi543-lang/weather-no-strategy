# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the owner and operator of a personal Polymarket weather research and paper-trading system. The dashboard is used repeatedly throughout the day to scan city forecasts, validate source freshness, inspect model and market changes, and audit what the AI decided and what the paper execution engine actually did.

## Product Purpose

Provide one operational view of the weather data pipeline and the three-thesis NO Paper system. Success means the user can quickly answer: which data changed, which cities were reviewed, whether AI identified `NO_OVERSHOOT`, `NO_CEILING`, or `NO_MARKET_TAIL_REJECTION`, whether it requested a BASE or STRONG position, whether the safety kernel executed it, and how each thesis performs after settlement.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, analytical, precise. The interface should feel like a serious compute and market operations console rather than a consumer weather app or a crypto promotion page.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing heroes, decorative illustrations, neon crypto styling, glassmorphism, oversized metrics, nested cards, and interfaces that rely on color alone. Do not turn research caveats or paper execution into celebratory trading language.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with current operational state, then expose detail progressively.
2. Keep AI reasoning, requested actions, and actual execution visibly separate.
3. Preserve timestamps and data provenance wherever a decision is shown.
4. Make WAIT and no-trade outcomes first-class, not empty or failed states.
5. Prefer dense tables, timelines, and restrained status labels over decorative cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, meaningful labels, and text alongside state colors. Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain readable contrast in the existing light gray interface.
